Taehui Yun

I am a graduate student in the Causality Lab at Seoul National University , advised by Prof. Sanghack Lee. I received my B.S. in Industrial and Management Engineering from POSTECH .

My research goal is to build convincing and safe AI systems grounded in causality. I am particularly interested in causal generative models, causal explainability (XAI), and time-series. Recently, I have been studying causal normalizing flows for time-series data and concept based model with causal lens. I enjoy deep-diving into diverse causal topics and collaborations. Please feel free to contact me if you share similar interests!
