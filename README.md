This is the website of Taehui Yun.

I am a graduate student in the Causality Lab at Seoul National University, advised by Sanghack Lee. 
I received my Bachelor's degree from the Department of Industrial and Management Engineering at POSTECH.

My research interests lie in building convincing and safe AI systems through the lens of causality. 
In particular, I study causal generative models and explainable AI (XAI) from a causal perspective. 
I am also interested in related topics such as causal discovery and time-series modeling.

Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in my work.
